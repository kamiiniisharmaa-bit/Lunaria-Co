1. Our commitment to privacy

Lunaria & Co. ("we", "us", "our") operates the website lunaria.co (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you visit our Site, make a purchase, or interact with our brand. We are a small, independent studio that values ethical practices — your data will never be sold, rented, or exploited.

Our promise: We only collect what we truly need to serve you better. No hidden agendas, no shady brokers.

2. Information we collect

We collect information to process orders, improve your experience, and communicate with you authentically. The categories include:

We do not collect sensitive personal data (e.g., health, biometric, or political information).

3. How we use your information

Your data helps us create a seamless, mindful shopping experience. Specifically, we use it for:

  • Order fulfillment: Process payments, ship products, provide invoices and order confirmations.
  • Customer support: Respond to inquiries, handle returns, and resolve issues with care.
  • Improving our craft: Analyze browsing trends to enhance product curation and site functionality.
  • Marketing (only with opt-in): Send occasional newsletters, early access offers, and journal posts if you subscribe. You can unsubscribe anytime.
  • Legal compliance: Detect fraud, enforce our Terms, or comply with applicable laws.

We never use automated decision-making or profiling that significantly affects you.

4. Sharing & third parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information. However, we work with trusted partners who help operate our business, subject to strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Shipping carriers: USPS, UPS, DHL (to deliver your order).
  • Payment processors: Stripe, PayPal, Shop Pay – they handle payment data securely.
  • E-commerce platform: Shopify (hosting and order management).
  • Analytics: Privacy-friendly analytics (Fathom or similar) to understand aggregate site usage without tracking individuals across the web.

We may also disclose information if required by law (e.g., court order) or to protect the rights and safety of Lunaria & Co., our customers, or others.

5. Cookies & tracking technologies

Our Site uses essential cookies to enable core functionality (like cart and checkout). We also use a small number of optional analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our pages — always anonymized and aggregated. You can manage cookie preferences via your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site performance.

🍪 Cookie-free zone: We never use tracking pixels from social media giants unless you explicitly consent. Your browsing stays peaceful.

Third-party embeds (like product videos) may set their own cookies, but we limit external services to protect your privacy.

6. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location (including GDPR for EEA/UK and CCPA for California residents), you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Ask us to erase your data (subject to legal obligations like tax records).
  • Restriction/Opt-out: Limit processing or opt out of marketing communications.
  • Data portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any right, email privacy@lunaria.co. We will respond within 30 days. We never discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

7. Data security & retention

We implement industry-standard safeguards including SSL encryption, secure servers, and restricted access to personal data. Only essential team members (founder + one operations lead) have access to order information.

We retain your data only as long as necessary: order records for 7 years (tax/legal compliance), newsletter data until you unsubscribe, and analytics for 14 months. After that, data is anonymized or deleted.

While no online transmission is 100% secure, we continuously update our practices to protect your information.

8. Children's privacy

Lunaria & Co. does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 years of age. Our products are designed for adults (home goods, apothecary, ceramics). If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9. Policy updates & contact

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Effective date" at the top indicates the latest revision. We will notify you of material changes via email or a prominent notice on our Site.
