our guiding threads

Artisan First

Every ceramic, textile, and botanical is made by hand — we partner directly with small-batch studios and pay fair, transparent wages.

Earth Stewardship

From recycled packaging to low-impact glazes, we tread lightly and prioritize regenerative materials across every touchpoint.

Slow Rituals

We believe objects should be cherished for years, not seasons. Our curation celebrates wabi-sabi, durability, and timeless design.

Elara Wren

Founder & Curator of Soulful Things

After a decade in fast fashion buying, Elara stepped away to reconnect with craft. She traveled through Portugal, Japan, and Mexico — learning from ceramicists, natural dyers, and weavers. In 2021, she founded Lunaria as a home for honest, poetic objects that honor both the maker and the user.

Today, she lives between a tiny coastal studio and an old farmhouse, where she tests new glazes and tends to a wildflower garden. Her greatest joy? Seeing a Lunaria piece become part of someone's daily quiet ritual.

“We don’t chase trends — we chase meaning.”
